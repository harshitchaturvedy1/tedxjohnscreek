OUR THEME FOR 2026 IS EVOLVE

At TEDx Johns Creek, EVOLVE is a call to thinkers, creators, and changemakers who are not just adapting to the future — they are actively shaping it. Through powerful ideas and lived experiences, we explore how growth begins within and radiates outward to transform families, communities, industries, and the world.

THE FIVE PILLARS OF EVOLVE

Scroll to explore each dimension of transformation

01 🔴

Personal Evolution

The inner journeys — overcoming fear, redefining identity, healing, learning, and discovering purpose. Every breakthrough begins with someone choosing to grow.

02 🔴

Social Evolution

How communities rethink systems, amplify unheard voices, and build more inclusive, compassionate societies.

03 🔴

Innovation & Ideas

New technologies, bold research, creative solutions, and unexpected perspectives that move humanity forward.

04 🔴

Leadership Evolution

From power to purpose. From authority to empathy. The leaders of tomorrow must evolve beyond titles into impact.

05 🔴

Resilience & Reinvention

Sometimes evolution isn't a choice — it's survival. Stories of setbacks turning into comebacks remind us that transformation is always possible.

  • Responsive Design: Fluid layouts that adapt to various screen sizes and zoom levels.
