ABOUT THE EVENT

A Stage for the Future

TEDxJohnsCreekYouth is an independently organized event that brings the spirit of TED to our local community. On September 19th, 2026, we will gather at Phase Events to witness the power of youth-led ideas. Our mission is to provide a world-class platform where the next generation of thinkers can share their unique perspectives and drive meaningful global change.

THE 2026 THEME

"One Spark Can Ignite a Movement."

The Spark is the beginning of everything. It's that fleeting moment of inspiration, the sudden realization, or the burning passion that refuses to be ignored. This year, we explore how individual "sparks" in Johns Creek are evolving into collective movements. From technology and art to social reform and science, we celebrate the ideas that are lighting the way toward the next evolution of our society.

Accessibility Features

We believe that ideas worth spreading should be accessible to everyone. This website has been built following WCAG 2.1 guidelines to ensure a high standard of accessibility:

  • Semantic HTML: Proper use of headings and landmarks for screen reader navigation.
  • Color Contrast: High contrast ratios for text and UI elements to aid visibility.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Fully navigable via keyboard with visible focus indicators.
  • Responsive Design: Fluid layouts that adapt to various screen sizes and zoom levels.
  • Alt Text: Meaningful descriptions for all functional and informative imagery.

If you encounter any barriers while using this site, please reach out to us at tedxjohnscreek@gmail.com.
